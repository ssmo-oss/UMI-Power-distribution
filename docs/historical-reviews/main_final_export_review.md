Reviewed outputs/UMI_TWO_BOARD_DESIGN/MAIN_POWER with read-only scripts work/review_main_exports.py and work/verify_cpl_angles.py. No deliverable edited.

BOM/CPL coverage passes:65 electrical parts,53 SMT positions,12 manual through-hole parts (six connectors and six fuseholders). Exploded grouped draft BOM, by-reference SMT BOM and CPL references agree exactly. All53 CPL coordinates/footprint angles match the filled board relative to auxiliary origin(10,110.263962). No additional electrical pin-assignment or polarity error found in the reviewed main controller, eFuse, power MOSFETs, catch diode, TVS and bulk capacitors.

One assembly-file correction was identified and communicated: many footprints bake a rotation into their local pad geometry, while raw KiCad CPL says0°. This is valid relative to those custom library variants but ambiguous when an assembler maps the MPN to its own library. Root is generating a normalized supplier file and retaining native raw export. Exact independently derived corrections are work/cpl_rotation_corrections.json. Every numbered pad in each rotated final footprint was compared with its unrotated source and matched the derived transform with zero coordinate error. Baked transform is clockwise in board-view coordinates, so normalized CCW angle is negative baked angle modulo360. U1 and Q1–Q3 become90°; C19/C20 become270°. Source footprint names should replace rotated variant names in the normalized file to prevent applying the transform twice. Supplier library zero-angle preview is still necessary.

Critical physical orientations: U1 pin1 lower-left of its final footprint; U2 pin1 upper-left, input EP25 above GND EP26; Q1/Q2/Q3 source pins1–3 below, gate4 bottom-right, drain land above; C19/C20 positive terminal above/negative below; D3 cathode left on fused input; D4 anodes above on GND and cathode below on BOOST_IN. eFuse PG13 routes UVLO, ITIMER7/NRETRY11 intentionally unconnected, RETRY_DLY10/LDSTRT12 grounded. Catch diode was relocated to(79,53) adjacent to eFuse(79,60), improving the previously flagged loop; dynamic−0.8V limit remains a bench test.
